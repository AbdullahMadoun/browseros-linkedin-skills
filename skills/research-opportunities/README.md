# Research Opportunity Skills

Skills for finding and pursuing credible AI/CS research opportunities, visiting programs, funded research internships, and professor/lab outreach routes.

| Skill | Use |
|---|---|
| `research-opportunity-finder` | Find credible research roles and programs with source-backed eligibility, deadlines, funding, and application routes. |
| `professor-lab-outreach-workflow` | Map professor/lab fit and prepare grounded outreach without duplicating contacts. |
