# Professor Lab Outreach Workflow

## Long-Term Memory

When durable memory is needed, use `obsidian-long-term-memory-workflow` to save it in an Obsidian vault. Keep chat and run notes transient unless they are linked from Obsidian.

## Purpose

Use this after a professor, lab, project, or research opportunity looks promising. The workflow creates credible outreach grounded in the user's actual background and the lab's work.

## Preflight

Before drafting:

- verify professor/lab name, institution, and official page
- identify research fit from current lab/project pages or papers
- check duplicate outreach memory
- identify the best contact route
- confirm the CV or document to attach if sending is requested

## Fit Map

Extract:

```text
Professor/lab:
Institution:
Research themes:
Relevant project/paper:
Why this user fits:
Specific contribution angle:
Contact route:
Recentness signal:
Risks or weak fit:
```

Use exact source links. Do not overstate familiarity with papers or projects.

## Draft Style

Default email style:

- short and serious
- English-only unless requested otherwise
- specific to the lab
- no long project-detail dump
- no exaggerated metrics or unsupported claims
- clear ask: research assistantship, visiting research, summer project, meeting, or guidance

## Safety

Do not send until:

- recipient is verified
- subject and body are final
- attachments are correct and visible if using web mail
- duplicate outreach check is complete
- user explicitly approves sending to that recipient

If the user asks only for drafts, stop at drafts.

## Tracking

After approved send or draft creation, record:

- professor/lab
- email or contact route, redacted in chat when appropriate
- date
- status: drafted, sent, follow-up due, replied, rejected, accepted
- source links and next action

Use Obsidian or the user's tracker when available.
